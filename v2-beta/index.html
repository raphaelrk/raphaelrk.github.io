Bible Codes — gbbl and Daaven

2024 · prototypes

gbbl implemented semantic search across the Chumash and wider Tanakh, accepting free-text queries or a verse reference and ranking related verses by embedding similarity. Compared English, Hebrew with niqqud, Hebrew with niqqud stripped, and combined English/Hebrew representations, as well as small and large embedding models. Results appeared side by side in English and Hebrew. Daaven separately processed the Ashkenazi prayerbook and displayed Amidah sections.
